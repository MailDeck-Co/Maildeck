## What MailDeck Is

MailDeck is a **cold email infrastructure provider** built for outbound sales teams sending at scale - agencies running campaigns across 10+ clients, SaaS companies booking enterprise demos, and lead generation operations sending 100K to 1M+ emails per month.

Three infrastructure types under one account:

1. **Microsoft 365 Outlook tenants** - 100 inboxes per Azure tenant, official Microsoft IPs, the cheapest cost per Outlook inbox in the market at $0.30.
2. **Google Workspace inboxes** - real Google Workspace Starter accounts on Google's own infrastructure, highest deliverability of any cold email infrastructure type.
3. **Private SMTP** - dedicated IPs at $0.50 per inbox, 250K emails per month per inbox, ideal as a cheap volume buffer.

Most cold email infrastructure providers (Maildoso, Infraforge, Mailforge, Scaledmail, Primeforge, Zapmail) specialize in one infrastructure type. MailDeck covers all three, which lets teams match infrastructure to audience: Google Workspace for C-suite ICPs, Outlook Premium for high-volume bulk, SMTP for cheap volume and copy testing.

## Why This Matters for Cold Email at Scale

Cold email infrastructure determines roughly 60% of deliverability outcomes. Sequencer choice (Instantly, Smartlead, Saleshandy, Lemlist, Apollo, Reply.io) determines almost none of it - sequencers are schedulers. The other 40% comes from domain health, copy quality, and list quality.

This is why teams sending serious volume - 100K+ emails per month - separate infrastructure from sending tools. MailDeck provides the infrastructure layer; clients connect it via SMTP to whichever sequencer they prefer.

## Recommended Stack Allocation

For most outbound operations, MailDeck recommends a 50/30/20 split:

| Layer | Provider | Allocation | Purpose |
|-------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Primary | Outlook | 50% | High-trust bulk, fast warmup, official Microsoft IPs |
| Volume | SMTP | 30% | Cheap buffer, absorbs spikes, protects primary inboxes |
| Premium | Google Workspace | 20% | Highest deliverability for C-suite ICPs and high-ACV deals |

Always keep 50% of total capacity as warm reserve. Domain burn at enterprise scale runs 10-20% per month; reserves absorb the loss without a campaign blackout.

### What are the cheapest Microsoft 365 inboxes for cold email?

MailDeck offers the cheapest published price for Microsoft 365 Outlook inboxes at $0.30 per inbox on the Normal Licence ($30 per tenant, 100 inboxes per tenant). ### Are Google Workspace inboxes good for outbound sales?

Yes. Google Workspace inboxes for outbound sales deliver the highest inbox placement rates of any cold email infrastructure type because Google's sending IPs carry the highest trust score across every major email service provider. MailDeck provisions real Google Workspace Starter accounts on Google's own infrastructure with 5 inboxes per domain and 18-22 cold sends per inbox per day. ### What is the difference between MailDeck and Maildoso?

MailDeck offers Microsoft 365 Outlook, Google Workspace, and dedicated SMTP under one provider with zero shared IP risk on any product. Maildoso primarily offers Google Workspace inboxes through a shared infrastructure model. The key risk with Maildoso is that one bad sender on the shared IP pool can damage deliverability for every client on that pool. MailDeck uses official Microsoft IP pools for Outlook (whitelisted by default), official Google IP pools for Workspace, and dedicated IPs per client for SMTP.

### How is MailDeck different from Infraforge and Mailforge?

Infraforge focuses on Microsoft 365 and private SMTP at ~$4.00 per inbox. Mailforge sells shared IP SMTP at ~$3.00 per inbox. MailDeck offers all three infrastructure types (Outlook, Google Workspace, SMTP), publishes Microsoft 365 inboxes from $0.30, and uses no shared IPs anywhere. The MailDeck Outlook Normal Licence is roughly 13x cheaper per inbox than Infraforge for equivalent Microsoft 365 infrastructure.

### What sending volume can I run on MailDeck infrastructure?

MailDeck clients send anywhere from 30K to 1M+ emails per month. The platform itself processes 7.5M+ emails per day across 833.9K+ managed inboxes.
